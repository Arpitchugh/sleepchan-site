Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

sleepchan is a personal tool operated by its owner for their own YouTube channel. It is not available to the public.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent via Google OAuth, sleepchan uploads videos, sets thumbnails, titles, descriptions, playlists and publish times on the owner's channel, and reads that channel's YouTube Analytics reports.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens and analytics data are stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. sleepchan's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which locally stored tokens stop working and are deleted.
