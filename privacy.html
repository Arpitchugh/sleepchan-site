Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026 · Operator: Ravix Studio (sole proprietorship of Arpit Chugh)

sleepchan is an internal tool operated by Ravix Studio for its own YouTube channel. It is not available to the public.

YouTube API Services

sleepchan uses YouTube API Services. By using sleepchan you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

Information accessed, collected and used

With the channel owner's consent through Google OAuth, sleepchan uploads videos and sets their thumbnails, titles, descriptions, localizations, playlists and publish times on the owner's channel, and reads that channel's YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, impressions, subscribers gained, estimated revenue). It also reads public search results (titles, view counts, channel subscriber counts) to rank topic ideas.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens and analytics data are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No cookies or device identifiers are placed on anyone's device. Public search data is used only to rank topic ideas and is refreshed or deleted within 30 days. sleepchan's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time via the Google security settings page at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. On revocation, or on request to the contact below, all stored tokens and data obtained from YouTube API Services are deleted within 7 days.
